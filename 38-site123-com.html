You do not need coding skills to put a best friend quiz on the internet. Modern website builders let you go from idea to a live, shareable quiz site in a single afternoon. All you need is a set of good questions and a free Saturday.

The key is to keep the scope tight. One page, one quiz, one clear call to action: take the test and share your score.

Pick Your Quiz Format

Decide on the structure before you touch the builder. The simplest format that works is a single scrolling page: an intro header, ten multiple-choice questions, and a results section at the bottom. Visitors answer as they scroll, which feels natural on phones.

Alternative formats work too. A personality-style quiz with no wrong answers suits sentimental friend groups, while a timed challenge fits competitive circles. Pick the format your friends will actually finish, not the fanciest one.

Write Questions That Feel Personal

Generic questions get generic engagement. The best quiz sites feel like they were made for one specific friend group, because they were. Write about real shared experiences: the trip, the tradition, the running joke only your circle understands.

When you create a friendship quiz for friends, aim for a mix of easy warm-ups, memory questions, and two or three curveballs. Ten questions is the sweet spot: enough to feel substantial, short enough to finish on a coffee break.

Publish and Share the Link

Once the page looks right, publish it and grab the link. Test it on your phone first, since that is where most friends will play. Check that buttons are tappable, text is readable, and the results section actually appears at the end.

Then share strategically for maximum participation:

  • Send it to your inner circle first to seed some scores
  • Post it in the group chat with a playful challenge attached
  • Ask the current high scorer to defend their title publicly
  • Set a deadline, like Sunday night, to create urgency

Watch the responses roll in and enjoy the arguments about who really knows you best. A quiz site you built yourself carries a personal touch no generic template can match.
