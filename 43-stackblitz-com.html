You no longer need paper, pens, or everyone in the same room to run a best friend quiz. With free online tools you can build the whole thing in an afternoon, share one link, and watch the scores roll in from friends anywhere in the world.

Pick Your Quiz Builder

Start with whatever tool feels easiest. Online form builders, quiz apps, and even a shared document all work. The best choice is the one your friends will actually open on their phones. Look for something that supports multiple choice questions, shows one question at a time, and calculates a score automatically.

Keep the setup minimal. A title, a short intro line, and your questions are enough. Fancy themes are nice, but nobody remembers the background color. They remember the questions.

Write Questions That Actually Test

Good online quizzes follow the same rules as paper ones. Mix easy warm-ups with memory questions only a real friend could answer:

  • Round one: favorites and daily habits
  • Round two: shared memories and inside jokes
  • Round three: predictions and wild cards

Write answer choices where two options sound plausible and one is a funny decoy. Avoid trick wording. The goal is to reward friends who pay attention, not to punish anyone.

Share the Link and Compare

Send the quiz link to your group chat and give everyone the same deadline. Once the answers are in, gather everyone on a call and reveal the results together. This is the best part: you get to compare friendship scores live, argue about the trickiest questions, and crown whoever knows you best.

Post a simple leaderboard so the competition feels real. The winner gets bragging rights until the next quiz.

Keep It Fair for Everyone

Make sure every question is answerable by everyone taking the quiz. If one friend joined the group later, skip the ancient history questions or add a round just for them. A fair quiz keeps everyone engaged, and an unfair one just creates eye rolls.

Building your quiz online takes a little setup, but the payoff is huge: one link, instant scoring, and a game night that runs itself.
